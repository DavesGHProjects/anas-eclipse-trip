Day 9 — Asturias at Leisure

Monday, August 17, 2026

San Lorenzo • Cimavilla • Sidra • One unhurried day in Gijón

After several ambitious travel days, this is the pause: a walkable day centered on the sea, the old town and Asturian food. The goal is not to complete a checklist—it is to enjoy Gijón at the pace of the city.

A relaxed working plan

MorningCoffee, San Lorenzo Beach promenade and a climb through Cimavilla to Cerro de Santa Catalina.
LunchA traditional sidrería: cider, shared Asturian dishes and enough time to enjoy the ritual.
AfternoonChoose one: Laboral Ciudad de la Cultura, a longer marina-and-shopping wander, or simply more beach time.
EveningA good seafood dinner, a final waterfront stroll and light packing for León.

Lunch — A Proper Sidrería

Let lunch be one of the day’s main events.

Sidra naturalFabadaCachopo to shareAsturian cheeses
For seven people: Sharing is the best strategy. Cachopo portions can be very large, and ordering several dishes gives everyone a broader taste of Asturias.

Vegetarian choices may include tortilla, cheeses, salads, croquetas and vegetable dishes, but confirm broths and fillings before ordering.

Stay in the Center

Marina, shops, cafés, beach time or a nap at the apartment. This is not the “lesser” option—it may be the best vacation choice.
