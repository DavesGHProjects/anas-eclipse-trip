Day 5 · Thursday, August 13

Post-Eclipse in Riaza

Something will happen today... wonder what? After yesterday’s birthday-and-eclipse extravaganza, a quieter Thursday in Riaza feels entirely appropriate.

The collective exhale

No totality. No frantic weather checks. No headless chickens.

Today is for sleeping a little later, comparing eclipse stories and photographs, wandering without urgency, and enjoying one more full day at La Casa Campo before the journey north resumes tomorrow.

Birthday policy: David’s 73½ birthday has been considered and rejected. Real birthdays only, please.
A gentle morning

Breakfast, debriefing and photographic evidence

The most likely first activity: reliving every minute of the eclipse before anyone has finished a second cup of coffee.

Compare photographs, trade stories about what each person noticed during totality, and decide whose memory of the previous evening is already the least reliable.

Sleep in Long breakfast Photo review Eclipse storytelling
Today’s only mission: choose a favorite eclipse photograph—or agree that the experience itself was better than any picture.
Option A

Stay very local

Stroll through Riaza, settle into a café, browse whatever catches the group’s attention, and allow the day to unfold without a schedule.

Option B

Do one small thing

Choose a short walk, a scenic drive, or another modest outing nearby—something pleasant enough to feel worthwhile, but not so ambitious that it competes with yesterday.

The shape of the day

Enough structure to prevent complete drift

MorningSleep late, eat slowly and revisit the eclipse from seven slightly different perspectives.
MiddayHead into Riaza for a wander, coffee, lunch or whatever seems appealing at the time.
AfternoonChoose between a brief local outing and the increasingly attractive option of doing almost nothing.
EveningEnjoy the final night at La Casa Campo and begin thinking about tomorrow’s departure for Santander.

Tomorrow: north to Santander
