Day 13 — Your Madrid Encore

Friday, August 21, 2026

One last full day. Catch up, choose your Madrid—or simply enjoy having nowhere to be until dinner.

Today has no required sightseeing. Pick the version of Madrid that sounds best after nearly two weeks on the road. The only hard stop is the grand finale: El Invernadero at 7:00 PM.

Madrid encore

Option A — Choose Your Madrid

Green Madrid
Retiro Park, the Crystal Palace area, shaded walks and a leisurely café.
Art Madrid
Choose one: Prado, Reina Sofía or Thyssen. One museum is an experience; three is a campaign.
Food Madrid
A market, an old taberna, chocolate con churros, or a long lunch—but keep it light enough to appreciate dinner.
Shopping Madrid
Gran Vía, Salamanca, neighborhood shops and the last souvenirs nobody remembered to buy.
Representative web image of Madrid.

Or mix-and-match

A park in the morning, one museum after lunch, a café in between—there is no prize for choosing a theme and sticking to it.

Because tonight's dinner is at 7:00 PM, build in a return to the Airbnb during the late afternoon. The best Day 13 itinerary is the one that leaves everyone refreshed rather than hurried when it is time to head for Chamberí.

Option B — The Lazy Catch-Up Day

No sightseeing required.

Sleep late. Edit pictures. Compare eclipse shots. Pack. Do laundry. Sit over coffee. Talk about the trip. Revisit a favorite neighborhood only if the mood strikes. After thirteen days together, this may be exactly the luxury everyone wants.

The Grand Finale

7:00 PM — El Invernadero

7:00 PM

El Invernadero — Rodrigo de la Calle
Calle de Ponzano, 85 · Chamberí, Madrid

After Castilian meals, Cantabrian seafood, Asturian cider culture and last night's hands-on tapas class, the trip ends its culinary story with Rodrigo de la Calle's gastrobotánica: modern fine dining built around the creative possibilities of vegetables and seasonal ingredients.

Protect the afternoon. Whatever Madrid option you choose, leave enough time to return to the Airbnb, recharge and arrive at El Invernadero without rushing.
