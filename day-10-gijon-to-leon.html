Day 10 — Gijón to León

Tuesday, August 18, 2026

One last Asturian adventure—or a leisurely arrival in León

Today has two legitimate versions. The direct route preserves a relaxed morning and a generous afternoon in León. The mountain route backtracks toward the Picos de Europa for scenery before turning south, and should be chosen only with an early start, favorable weather and good group energy.

Relaxed choice

Option A — Direct to León

Breakfast in Gijón → checkout → León by lunchtime or early afternoon

This is the best option for enjoying León rather than merely sleeping there. Arrive after the hotel’s 2:00 PM check-in begins, settle the cars and rooms, then explore the cathedral quarter and old town on foot.

Best balance: Because Day 8 already includes Covadonga on the featured route, the direct drive may provide the most varied and least tiring trip.
Weather-dependent

Option B — Backtrack to the Picos

Early departure → mountain scenery/Covadonga area → León

Use this option if Covadonga was skipped or abbreviated on Day 8, or if the family wants a deeper mountain experience. Choose one focused objective—such as Cangas de Onís, the sanctuary area or the Covadonga Lakes—and avoid building an overstuffed loop.

Access warning: Summer access to the Covadonga Lakes is regulated and may require shuttle transport. Confirm the current traffic plan, parking and operating hours before committing to this route.

Afternoon and Evening in León

Stained glass, historic streets and one of Spain’s best traditions of tapas with drinks.

León CathedralBarrio HúmedoCasa BotinesEvening tapas

Start with the cathedral if opening hours permit, then wander toward Casa Botines and the old center. For dinner, explore the Barrio Húmedo and Barrio Romántico, where the pleasure is in moving between a few places rather than committing immediately to one long meal.

León weather at a glance

León is likely to feel warmer and drier than coastal Asturias during the afternoon, with a noticeable temperature drop after sunset. Keep both sun protection and a light evening layer handy.

Morning decision checklist

Check mountain weather, regulated access and expected arrival time in León. If the Picos route risks a late hotel arrival or leaves no meaningful time in León, take the direct route without regret.
