Choose your Santander beach

All four choices are reached most easily by car or taxi from the Ibis Styles Santander on Avenida de Parayas.

Best all-around choice: Start with Playa del Sardinero. It offers the broadest sand, the most services and the classic Santander waterfront experience. The other beaches work well when the group wants calmer water, scenery or a quieter cove.

Playa del Sardinero

Best overall. Santander's signature urban beach has broad golden sand, a handsome promenade, easy access and plenty of nearby cafés and restaurants. It is the easiest choice for a full, uncomplicated beach outing.

A simple beach-day strategy

For maximum ease

Go directly to Sardinero, choose a convenient stretch of sand and have lunch nearby. This avoids moving the cars repeatedly and gives everyone freedom to wander, swim or sit at a café.

For more variety

Begin with a walk around the Magdalena Peninsula, spend time at Magdalena or Peligros, then drive along the coast past Sardinero before returning to the hotel.

August reality: Parking near the beaches may be busy. Leaving after a leisurely but not overly late breakfast should make the day easier, and taxis are a reasonable alternative if nobody wants to manage parking.

The evening's main event

🎂 Norita's Birthday at El Serbal

A polished 8:30 PM dinner celebrating Norita and the produce, flavors and culinary imagination of Cantabria.

Reservation: Dinner is planned for 8:30 PM. Aim to leave the hotel with enough time for parking or a taxi ride and to arrive calmly a few minutes before the reservation.

El Serbal describes its cuisine as refined and creative while remaining grounded in the products and traditional flavors of Cantabria. This is the day's fixed commitment, so the beach plan should remain comfortably secondary.
