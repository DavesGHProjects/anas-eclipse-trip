Saturday, August 22, 2026

Two departures, one possible airport shuttle run, and perhaps a tiny Madrid epilogue.

Everyone flies today, but not at the same time. The plan depends partly on the Airbnb's actual checkout time, so confirm that before deciding whether one car can comfortably make both airport runs.

If checkout is late enough

The two-run plan

David takes the Miami travelers to MAD first, then returns to the Airbnb. The later-departing group gets a few more hours to finish packing, have breakfast or take a short walk before the second airport run.

This is the most convenient version if the Airbnb permits occupancy late enough and the vehicle arrangements make a return trip sensible.

If checkout is early

Move everyone out once

Check out together, take the first group to the airport, and let the later travelers spend the remaining hours somewhere easy with luggage handled appropriately. Possibilities include a relaxed breakfast or lunch near a luggage-storage option, or simply continuing to the airport earlier than ideal and settling in there.

Do not assume: the Airbnb checkout time is still unknown. Treat the two-run plan as an option until the host's checkout instructions are confirmed.

A tiny Madrid epilogue?

If logistics cooperate, the Santa Barbara group may have time for one last café con leche, pastry, neighborhood walk or final purchase after the Miami airport run. Keep it very local and very optional—the objective today is an unhurried departure, not one final sightseeing achievement.

The road-trip loop is complete

Madrid → Riaza → Santander → Gijón → León → Madrid, with birthdays, the eclipse, caves, beaches, mountains, cooking, extraordinary meals and a great many kilometers in between.

Hasta luego, España.
